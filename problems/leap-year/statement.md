# Check Leap Year

Print Yes for a leap year, otherwise No. A year is leap if divisible by 400, or divisible by 4 but not 100.

## Input

One integer year.

## Output

Print Yes for a leap year, otherwise No. A year is leap if divisible by 400, or divisible by 4 but not 100.

## Constraints

1 ≤ year ≤ 9999.
